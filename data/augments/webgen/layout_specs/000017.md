# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport, full width — wordmark "Fundraiser Day" at header-left; nav links "About", "Offers", "Testimonials", "Company" in a horizontal row at header-right.
- Main content: below the header, single centered column.
  - Hero section (top): heading "Welcome to Fundraiser Day 2026" at section-center-top; one short paragraph of descriptive subtext directly below the heading, center-aligned ("A nationwide celebration empowering communities to give, grow, and gather."); a primary CTA button labeled "Explore Offers" centered below the subtext.
  - Exclusive Offers section (upper-middle): section heading "Exclusive Offers" at section-top-center. Below it, a carousel card spanning most of the column width, centered horizontally. Inside the card: a small badge/tag at card-top-center (e.g. "Schools"); a bold offer title centered below the badge ("Double Donation Match"); a short description line centered below the title ("Every dollar you donate on Fundraiser Day is matched 2-to-1 by our partners, up to $500 per donor."). A left-arrow control sits at middle-left of the carousel (outside or on the card edge), a right-arrow control at middle-right. A row of four pagination dots sits at card-bottom-center (first dot active/filled, others outlined).
  - Fundraiser Stories section (middle): section heading "Fundraiser Stories" at section-top-center. Below it, three testimonial cards arranged in a single horizontal row (three columns of equal width). Each card contains: an italic quoted testimonial paragraph at card-top; a name in bold at card-bottom-left of the text block; a role/title line directly under the name. Cards from left to right: "Maria Gonzalez / School PTA Organizer", "David Park / Animal Shelter Director", "Aisha Khan / Nonprofit Founder".
  - About Our Company section (lower-middle): section heading "About Our Company" at section-top-center. Below it, three columns arranged in a single row:
    - first column (left): subheading "Our Mission" at column-top-left; a short paragraph of body text below it.
    - second column (center): subheading "By the Numbers" at column-top-left; a vertical bulleted list of three stat lines below it ("$400+ raised across 12 years", "3,200+ nonprofits supported", "180,000+ active donors worldwide").
    - third column (right): subheading "Contact" at column-top-left; a stacked block of contact lines below it (street address line, city/state/zip line, email line, phone line).
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width — single line of small text centered ("© 2026 Fundraiser Day. All rights reserved.") at footer-center.
- Interactive elements:
  - Top-right header nav links: "About", "Offers", "Testimonials", "Company".
  - Hero primary CTA button "Explore Offers" at hero-bottom-center.
  - Carousel previous-arrow control at carousel-middle-left; next-arrow control at carousel-middle-right; four pagination dots at carousel-bottom-center.
