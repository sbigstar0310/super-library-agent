# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport, full width — square "IP" badge with "Internship Portal" wordmark at header-left; nav buttons "Home" (active, pill-highlighted), "Student Panel", "Company Panel" in a row at header-right.
- Main content: below header, vertically stacked, content centered horizontally.
  - Hero banner (top of main, near full-width card): heading "Launch your career with the right internship." at banner-top-center; supporting paragraph "The Internship Portal connects ambitious students with companies offering hands-on opportunities. Browse openings, apply in minutes, and track your progress in one place." at banner-middle-center; two buttons in a row at banner-bottom-center — "I'm a Student" (light, primary affordance) and "I'm a Company" (dark, secondary affordance).
  - Stats row (middle of main): three equal cards side by side. First card (row-left): large number "3" at card-top-center, label "Active internships" at card-bottom-center. Second card (row-center): "3" / "Partner companies". Third card (row-right): "4 mo" / "Avg. duration".
  - "How it works" section (bottom of main): section heading "How it works" at section-top-center; three equal step cards in a row beneath. Each card has a small numbered circle ("1", "2", "3") at card-top-center, a step title at card-middle-center ("Browse", "Apply", "Connect"), and a one-line description at card-bottom-center ("Filter internships by role, location, and skills.", "Submit a quick application — no lengthy forms.", "Hear back from companies and track your status.").
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width — text "© 2026 Internship Portal · Connecting students & companies" at footer-center.

## Screen: home-via-brand
- Identical visible layout to the `home` screen: same header (Home pill active at header-right), same hero banner at main-top, same three-card stats row at main-middle, same "How it works" three-step row at main-bottom, same footer text at footer-center.

## Screen: student-browse
- Header: top of viewport, full width — "IP" badge + "Internship Portal" wordmark at header-left; nav "Home", "Student Panel" (pill-highlighted active), "Company Panel" at header-right.
- Main content: below header, single column constrained to page-center.
  - Page heading block (main-top-left): title "Student Panel" at top, subtitle "Find and apply for internships that match your skills." directly beneath.
  - Tab bar (below heading): "Browse internships" (active, underlined) at tabs-left, "My applications (0)" to its right; horizontal divider beneath the tabs.
  - Filter row (below tabs): text input "Search by role, company, or skill…" at row-left (wider), a dropdown showing "All" at row-right (narrower).
  - Internship list (stacked cards, main-middle to main-bottom). Each card has:
    - Title (e.g., "Frontend Developer Intern", "Data Science Intern", "UX Design Intern") at card-top-left, with a pill badge showing stipend (e.g., "$1,200/mo") at card-top-right.
    - Meta line "<Company> · <Location> · <Duration>" at card-upper-left beneath the title.
    - One-line role description at card-middle-left.
    - Skill chips in a row at card-middle-left below the description (e.g., "React", "CSS", "JavaScript").
    - "Apply now" button at card-bottom-left; "Posted YYYY-MM-DD" text at card-bottom-right.
- Footer: bottom of viewport — "© 2026 Internship Portal · Connecting students & companies" at footer-center.

## Screen: student-apply-form
- Header: identical to student-browse header; "Student Panel" pill active at header-right.
- Main content: same Student Panel heading block at main-top-left, same tab bar with "Browse internships" active, same filter row beneath.
- Internship list: first card ("Frontend Developer Intern") is expanded — title/stipend/meta/description/skill chips at card-top in the same arrangement, then an inline application form occupying the card-middle through card-bottom:
  - Text input "Full name" at form-top, full width.
  - Text input "alice@example.com" beneath, full width.
  - Text input "Resume URL (optional)" beneath, full width.
  - Multi-line textarea "Short cover note (optional)" beneath, full width.
  - Buttons row at form-bottom-left: filled "Submit application" primary button, with outlined "Cancel" button immediately to its right.
- Subsequent cards ("Data Science Intern", "UX Design Intern") below are in their collapsed default form with "Apply now" at card-bottom-left and posted date at card-bottom-right.
- Footer: "© 2026 Internship Portal · Connecting students & companies" at footer-center.

- Footer: "© 2026 Internship Portal · Connecting students & companies" at footer-center (full-width band).
