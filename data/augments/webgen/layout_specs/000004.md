- Header (top of viewport): wordmark "🏀 Hoops Analytics" at header-left, tagline below.
- Nav bar (below header): six pill buttons; "Betting Picks" active.
- Main content: two stacked full-width panels.
  - Panel 1 "Betting Recommendations" (top of main): heading at panel-top with divider below; sub-line "Comparing model win probability against sportsbook implied odds to spot value." at panel-upper-left. Below, a vertical stack of four inset pick cards. Each pick card contains:
