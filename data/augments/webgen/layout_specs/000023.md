# Layout spec

## Screen: advice
- Header: top of viewport, full width — "Sunrise CRM" wordmark at header-top-left; below it at header-bottom-left a horizontal row of nav items: "Leads", "Messages", "Reports", "Settings" rendered as buttons, and "Advice" rendered as plain text (indicating current screen).
- Main content: page-center area split into two equal-width bordered cards in one row.
  - Left card (middle-left): title "Best Practices" at card-top. Below, a vertical stack of five entries, each a bold heading followed by a short paragraph, separated by horizontal divider lines. Headings in order: "Respond within 5 minutes", "Qualify before pitching", "Personalize every message", "Track every interaction", "Review weekly metrics".
  - Right card (middle-right): title "Recommended Integrations" at card-top. Below, vertical stack of five entries with bold heading + paragraph, separated by dividers. Headings in order: "Email (SMTP / Gmail / Outlook)", "Calendar", "VoIP / Telephony", "Marketing Automation", "Accounting".
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width, centered text "CRM Demo · Data stored locally".
