# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full-width indigo bar): "GIIS" badge at header-left followed by wordmark "Global Institute of Information Security"; at header-right a horizontal nav row — Home (highlighted/active), Courses, Certifications, My Enrollments, Contact, "Hi, admin", Logout.
- Main content (below header, page-center, lavender background):
  - Hero banner (top, full-width indigo card): title "Global Institute of Information Security" at banner-top-center; tagline "World-class training in cybersecurity, ethical hacking, cloud security, and incident response. Earn industry-recognized certifications online, at your own pace." centered below; at banner-bottom-center a row of two buttons — "Browse Courses" (primary) and "View Certifications" (secondary).
  - Section "Featured Courses" (middle-upper): heading at section-top-left; three course cards in a single row. Each card: level tag (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced) at card-top-left, course title below, short description below that, and a full-width "View Details" button at card-bottom. Cards in order: Cybersecurity Fundamentals, Network Security, Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing.
  - Section "Why Choose GIIS" (middle): heading at section-top-left; three cards in a row — "Expert Instructors" (Industry practitioners with decades of frontline security experience.), "Recognized Certifications" (Our credentials are recognized by leading organizations worldwide.), "Hands-On Labs" (Real-world labs and simulations to sharpen practical skills.).
  - Section "Certification Pathways" (middle-lower): heading at section-top-left; four cards arranged as two cards in the first row and two below (3-column grid with the fourth card on a new row): "Foundations" with badge "GIIS Certified Security Associate (GCSA)", "Defensive Security" with badge "GIIS Certified Defender (GCD)", "Offensive Security" with badge "GIIS Certified Penetration Tester (GCPT)", "Blue Team" with badge "GIIS Certified Incident Handler (GCIH)". Each card has a title at card-top-left, a one-line description, and a pill badge near card-bottom-left.
- Floating chat button: "Chat with us" at bottom-right of viewport, anchored above footer.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full-width indigo bar): "© 2026 Global Institute of Information Security" centered.
