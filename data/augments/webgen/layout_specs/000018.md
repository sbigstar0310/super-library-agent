# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full width): wordmark "✦ Sparkle Home Cleaning" at header-left; horizontal nav at header-right with links in order — Home, Services, Book Now, Service Inquiry, Check Status, Contact Us. The "Home" item is rendered as the active/selected affordance (boxed/inverted).
- Main content (below header, center-aligned column with left/right gutters):
  - Hero card (top of main): heading "A Sparkling Clean Home, Effortlessly" at card-top-center; supporting paragraph "Professional, trusted home and office cleaning. Book in minutes, pay only after we shine your space." centered below the heading; two buttons in a row at card-bottom-center — primary "Book a Cleaning" (filled) on the left, secondary "Browse Services" (outlined) on the right.
  - Feature row (middle of main): three equal-width cards side-by-side. Each card has a bold title at card-top-left and a one-line description below it.
    1. "Trained Professionals" — "Background-checked, insured, and trained to the highest standards."
    2. "Eco-Friendly Products" — "Plant-based, non-toxic supplies that are safe for kids and pets."
    3. "100% Satisfaction" — "Not happy? We'll come back and re-clean — free of charge."
  - Section heading "Popular Services" at main-left below the feature row.
  - Popular services row (bottom of main): three equal-width cards side-by-side. Each card has:
    - Title at card-top-left (e.g., "Standard Cleaning").
    - Price line "From $XX" below the title.
    - Multi-line description paragraph below the price.
    - "Learn more →" link at card-bottom-left.
    Cards in order: "Standard Cleaning / From $89", "Deep Cleaning / From $189", "Move In / Out Cleaning / From $249".
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full width, dark band): single centered line "© 2026 Sparkle Home Cleaning · ☎ (555) 123-4567 · ✉ support@sparkleclean.com".
