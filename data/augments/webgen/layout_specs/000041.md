# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full width): wordmark "Trading Hub" at header-left; nav buttons in a row at header-right in order: Home (active/highlighted), Browse, Account, History, admin (pill-style badge), Logout.
- Main content (page-center, top region under header): one wide card occupies the full content width.
  - Card top-left: heading "Welcome to Trading Hub".
  - Below heading: subtitle text "Bid on rare collectibles, place trades, and manage your portfolio — all in one place."
  - Below subtitle, a row of two buttons at card-left: primary "Browse Listings" then secondary "Create Account".
  - Card-bottom: three equal-width feature tiles in a single row.
    - First tile (left): title "Live Bidding"; body "Place real-time bids on hot items."
    - Second tile (center): title "Trade Orders"; body "Buy instantly at current prices."
    - Third tile (right): title "Account Tools"; body "Manage funds and review history."
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full width): centered text "Trading Hub © 2026".

## Screen: auth-login
- Header (top of viewport): wordmark "Trading Hub" at header-left; nav buttons at header-right in order: Home, Browse, Login, Register.
- Main content (top-center under header): single centered card.
  - Card-top: two-tab toggle row — "Login" (active, light fill) at left, "Register" (inactive, dark fill) at right.
  - Below tabs (form area, stacked top-to-bottom):
    - Label "Username or Email" → text input (placeholder "admin or admin@example.com").
    - Label "Password" → password input.
    - Full-width submit button labeled "Login" at card-bottom.
- Footer (bottom of viewport): centered text "Trading Hub © 2026".

## Screen: browse
- Header (top of viewport): wordmark "Trading Hub" at header-left; nav buttons at header-right in order: Home, Browse (active/highlighted), Account, History, admin (pill badge), Logout.
- Main content (page-center under header): wide card filling content width.
  - Card-top-left: heading "Trading Opportunities".
  - Below heading: filter row — wide search text input (placeholder "Search items...") at left taking most of the width, category dropdown labeled "All" at right.
  - Below filter row: responsive grid of listing cards, four per row in the first row, two in the second row.
    - Each listing card contains:
      - Top-left: item title (e.g. "Vintage Gold Coin"); top-right: category tag pill (e.g. "Collectibles", "Art", "Sports", "Fashion").
      - Stacked text lines: "Start: $<n>", "Current Bid: $<n>" (bold value), "By: <user>", "Ends in: <duration>".
      - Bid row: numeric input (prefilled with a bid amount) at left, "Bid" button at right.
      - Buy row: quantity input (prefilled "1") at left, "Buy Now" button at right.
  - Listings shown: Vintage Gold Coin (Collectibles), Antique Pocket Watch (Collectibles), Modern Art Painting (Art), Signed Baseball (Sports), Rare Comic Book (Collectibles), Designer Handbag (Fashion).
- Footer (bottom of viewport): centered text "Trading Hub © 2026".
