- Header (top of viewport, full width, medium-violet-red band): wordmark "E-Government System" at header-left; at header-right, the text "Director Chen ·" followed by a "Reset Demo Data" button.
- Sidebar (middle-left, full height under header, light pink background): vertical nav with three grouped sections, each preceded by a small uppercase group label.
  - Group "OVERVIEW" (sidebar-top): items "Dashboard", "Status Query".
  - Group "MANAGEMENT" (sidebar-middle): items "Office", "Infrastructure", "Procurement", "Personnel", "Financial", "Decision-Making".
  - Group "SPECIAL WORKFLOWS" (sidebar-bottom): items "Leave", "Cadres Leaving Shenzhen", "Attendance", "Five Powers".
  - Active item is highlighted with a pink fill and a left edge marker.
- Main content: middle-right of viewport, light-pink page background, content split into stacked white rounded cards.
