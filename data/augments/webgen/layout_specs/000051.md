# Layout spec

Shared chrome appears on every screen:
- Header: navy bar across the top of the viewport. Header-left: wordmark "DriverHire". Header-center-left (immediately right of the wordmark): horizontal nav links in order — Home, About Us, Clients, Drivers, Jobs, Contact; the link matching the current screen has an underline. Header-right: when signed in, plain-text label "Test Driver" followed by a light "Sign out" pill; when signed out, a single light "Sign in" pill.
- Footer: navy bar across the bottom of the viewport. Footer-left: "© 2026 DriverHire. All rights reserved." Footer-right: "Connecting drivers with great employers."

## Screen: home
- Header: as shared. "Home" is the active (underlined) nav item; the right side shows "Test Driver" and "Sign out".
- Main content: vertically stacked, page-center column.
  - Top: a wide navy hero block spanning the main column.
    - Hero-top-left: heading "Hire the road's best drivers."
    - Hero-middle-left (below heading): two-line paragraph "DriverHire connects companies with qualified CDL drivers across the country. Post a job in minutes or browse openings tailored to your experience."
    - Hero-bottom-left: a row of two buttons — primary-style "Browse Jobs" (white fill) on the left, outlined "Post a Job" on the right.
  - Below the hero: a three-column row of equal-width outlined cards on a white background.
    - First column (row-left): "For Drivers" heading at card-top-left, body "Create a profile, save jobs, and apply with a single click." at card-middle-left.
    - Second column (row-center): "For Clients" heading at card-top-left, body "Reach pre-screened drivers and manage your postings in one place." at card-middle-left.
    - Third column (row-right): "Trusted Network" heading at card-top-left, body "Thousands of placements across freight, delivery, and transit." at card-middle-left.
- Footer: as shared.
