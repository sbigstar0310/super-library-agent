# Layout spec

Global chrome (present on every screen):
- Sidebar: anchored to viewport-left, full height, dark red background.
  - Sidebar-top: square "BT" badge at sidebar-left next to wordmark "BuildTrack" with subtitle "Construction Mgmt" stacked beneath.
  - Sidebar-middle (vertical nav, stacked top-to-bottom): Dashboard, Projects, Schedule, Documents, Communication, Estimates & Costing, Time Tracking, Resources. Active item shown as a lighter red filled row spanning full sidebar width.
  - Sidebar-bottom: small text "v1.0 — local only" at sidebar-bottom-left.
- Main content: occupies area right of sidebar on a light gray background. Each screen begins with a page heading at top-left in dark red and a one-line subtitle directly beneath it.

## Screen: dashboard
- Header (page-top-left): "Dashboard" title; subtitle "Overview of all active construction work." beneath.
- Main content: below header.
  - Top row (four equal-width KPI cards in one row, page-top spanning full main width): each card has an uppercase label at card-top-left and a large numeric value at card-middle-left. From left to right:
    1. ACTIVE PROJECTS — 2
    2. TOTAL BUDGET — $5750k
    3. HOURS LOGGED — 15.5
    4. OPEN TASKS — 2
  - Second row (three equal-width panels in one row, below KPI row):
    - Left panel "Project Progress": title at panel-top-left, outlined "View all" button at panel-top-right. Body lists two projects stacked vertically; each item has project name at row-left with a status pill ("In Progress", "Planning") at row-right, a horizontal progress bar beneath, and a small line "NN% complete · Client: ..." below.
    - Middle panel "Recent Communication": title at panel-top-left. Body lists two messages stacked: each item shows author name (bold) with timestamp beside it, then the message text on the next line. A thin divider separates the entries.
    - Right panel "Upcoming Tasks": title at panel-top-left. Body lists two tasks stacked: task name (bold) on top line, then a meta line "<Project> · <start> → <end> · <assignee>" beneath. Thin divider between entries.
- Interactive elements: "View all" button at top-right of Project Progress panel.
