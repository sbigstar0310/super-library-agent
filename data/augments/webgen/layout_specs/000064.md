# Layout spec

Shared chrome (present on every screen):
- Header (top of viewport, full width): at header-left, a circular anchor icon followed by the wordmark "Maritime Wire" with the italic tagline "Shipping news, companies & events" beneath it. Below the wordmark, a horizontal nav row at header-bottom-left with buttons in order: Home, News, Companies, Events, Newsletter, Admin. The button matching the current screen is rendered as the active/filled state; the others are outlined.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full width): centered text "Maritime Wire — Shipping Industry News & Intelligence" on the first line and "© 2026 Maritime Wire. All rights reserved." on the second line, both at footer-center.

## Screen: home
- Header: shared chrome; Home button shown active at header-bottom-left.
- Main content: center column.
  - Hero panel (top of main): title "The pulse of global shipping" at panel-top-center, italic subtitle "Daily news, company intelligence and event coverage for the maritime industry." below it at panel-center, and a row of two buttons at panel-bottom-center — primary "Read latest news" on the left and secondary "Subscribe" on the right.
  - Sponsored ad panel (below hero, dashed outline): at panel-top-left a small "SPONSORED" tag, beneath it the advertiser name "Lloyd's Register" as a heading, and below that the placement label "Homepage Banner · Top of Home".
  - "Top headlines" panel (below ad): heading at panel-top-left, then three list rows stacked. Each row shows a date chip at row-left (e.g. `2026-05-18`), the headline at row-center-left, and a category label in italics at row-right (Trade Routes, Regulation, Green Shipping).
  - "Upcoming events" panel (below headlines): heading at panel-top-left, then three list rows. Each row: date chip at row-left, event name at row-center-left, location in italics at row-right (Athens, Greece / Oslo, Norway / Hamburg, Germany).

## Screen: news
- Header: shared chrome; News button shown active.
- Main content: center column.
  - Page title "Industry News" at top-left of main.
  - Filter chip row below title at middle-left: All (active/filled), Trade Routes, Regulation, Green Shipping, Markets.
  - Article grid below filters: three cards per row, wrapping; a fourth card begins a second row at row-left.
    - Each card has, in order from card-top: a small category tag (e.g. "TRADE ROUTES", "REGULATION", "GREEN SHIPPING", "MARKETS"), the article title as a heading, a date line (e.g. `2026-05-18`), a short summary paragraph, and a "Read full story →" link at card-bottom-left.

## Screen: news-detail
- Header: shared chrome; News button shown active.
- Main content: a single centered card (page-center-top).
  - Card-top row: at card-top-left a button labeled "← Back to news", to its right a category tag "TRADE ROUTES".
  - Below that, article heading "Panama Canal Eases Draft Restrictions as Rainy Season Returns" at card-left, then date `2026-05-18` beneath the heading.
  - Below the date, a body paragraph of article text fills the remaining card width.
- Rest of viewport below the card is empty space down to the footer.

## Screen: companies
- Header: shared chrome; Companies button shown active.
- Main content: center column.
  - Page title "Company Profiles" at top-left of main.
  - Full-width search input below title with placeholder "Search by name or HQ…".
  - Card grid below search: three cards per row, wrapping; a fourth card begins a second row at row-left.
    - Each card shows, from card-top: company name as a heading, an italic HQ line with a small marker (e.g. "Oslo, Norway"), an italic fleet line (e.g. "Bulk carriers, 48 vessels"), and a descriptive paragraph at card-bottom.

## Screen: events
- Header: shared chrome; Events button shown active.
- Main content: center column.
  - Page title "Industry Events" at top-left of main.
  - Vertical stack of full-width event rows below the title. Each row is a card containing:
    - At card-left, a date block with the day number on top (e.g. "02") and the year-month beneath it (e.g. `2026-06`).
    - To the right of the date block: event title as a heading at top, italic location line beneath (e.g. "Athens, Greece"), then a one-line description below.
  - Rows shown top-to-bottom: Posidonia 2026, Nor-Shipping Decarbonisation Summit, SMM Hamburg, TPM Asia.

## Screen: newsletter
- Header: shared chrome; Newsletter button shown active.
- Main content: a single centered card at page-top-center (rest of viewport empty above footer).
  - Card-top-center: heading "Maritime Wire Daily".
  - Below heading at card-center: descriptive paragraph "Get the day's top shipping stories, market moves, and event coverage delivered to your inbox each morning."
  - Subscribe form row below description: email input (placeholder "you@company.com") at row-left and a "Subscribe" button at row-right.
  - Below the form at card-bottom-center: italic status text "0 subscribers and counting."

## Screen: admin
- Header: shared chrome; Admin button shown active.
- Main content: center column.
  - Page title "Ad Space Management" at top-left of main, with an italic descriptive subtitle beneath: "Administer the ad inventory shown across Maritime Wire. Active spots with an assigned advertiser appear on the homepage."
  - Stats row below subtitle: three equal-width stat tiles side by side — left tile "3 / total spaces", center tile "2 / active", right tile "$6,300 / monthly revenue". Each tile shows the large number at tile-top-center and the label at tile-bottom-center.
  - "New ad space" form panel below stats: heading at panel-top-left, then a two-column grid of labeled inputs:
    - Row 1: Name → text input (left); Placement → text input (right).
    - Row 2: Price (USD/mo) → text input (left); Advertiser → text input with placeholder "(empty if unsold)" (right).
    - Row 3: a checkbox labeled "Active" at row-left (checked by default).
    - Submit "Create space" button at panel-bottom-left.
  - "Current inventory" table panel below the form: heading at panel-top-left, then a table with column headers NAME, PLACEMENT, ADVERTISER, PRICE, STATUS, and an unlabeled actions column at the far right. Three data rows:
    - "Homepage Banner | Top of Home | Lloyd's Register | $4,500 | Active (badge) | Edit, Delete buttons"
    - "News Sidebar | News page sidebar | Wilhelmsen Ship Service | $1,800 | Active (badge) | Edit, Delete"
    - "Events Footer | Bottom of Events | unsold (italic) | $950 | Paused (badge) | Edit, Delete"
