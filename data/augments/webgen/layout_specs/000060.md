# Layout spec

Global chrome present on every screen:
- Header: top of viewport, full width — at header-left a square "VM" tile next to wordmark "VocabMaster"; at header-center a horizontal nav row of text links "Home, Dashboard, Flashcards, Crossword, Unjumble, Syn/Ant, Fill Blank, Waiting Room, Blog" (the link matching the current screen is shown as a pill/active tag); at header-right either a single "Sign in" button (logged-out) or a circular avatar swatch + "admin" label + "Log out" button (logged-in).
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width, centered text "© 2026 VocabMaster — Adult vocabulary, made playful."

## Screen: auth-login
- Header: as above, header-right shows "Sign in" button only.
- Main content: page-center — a single bordered card with an accent bar across its top edge.
  - Card-top: heading "Sign in to VocabMaster".
  - Below heading: a two-button segmented control in one row — "Log in" (primary, active) at left, "Register" (secondary) at right.
  - Form (stacked, full card width):
    - Label "Username or email" → text input (focused outline visible).
    - Label "Password" → text input.
    - Submit button "Log in" (primary, full width).
  - Card-bottom: small helper line "Demo account: admin / admin@example.com / admin123456" with the three values shown as inline code chips.

## Screen: auth-register
- Header: same as auth-login (logged-out, "Sign in" at header-right).
- Main content: page-center — bordered card with accent top bar.
  - Card-top: heading "Create your account".
  - Segmented control row: "Log in" (secondary) at left, "Register" (primary, active) at right.
  - Form:
    - Label "Username" → text input.
    - Label "Email" → text input.
    - Label "Password" → text input.
    - Submit button "Create account" (primary, full width).
  - Card-bottom: same "Demo account: admin / admin@example.com / admin123456" helper line.

## Screen: home
- Header: logged-in variant; "Home" pill active.
- Main content: two-column row at the top of the page.
  - Left column (≈⅔ width): heading "Build an adult vocabulary you'll actually use.", a paragraph of descriptive text below, then a row of two buttons — "Open Dashboard" (primary) and "Read the blog" (secondary).
  - Right column (≈⅓ width): bordered card with accent top bar — "This week — Week 1" heading at card-top, italic "Theme: Eloquence & Expression" below, then a wrapped row of 10 pill tags (eloquent, verbose, laconic, candid, cogent, pithy, banal, erudite, aplomb, sagacious) filling the card.
- Mid section: heading "Five ways to drill the same ten words" left-aligned, then a horizontal row of 5 equal-width cards (Flashcards, Crossword, Unjumble, Syn/Ant, Fill the Blank) — each card has a title at card-top and a short one-line description below.
- Lower section: full-width bordered banner with accent top bar — at banner-left a heading "Waiting on a quiz to load? Play in the lobby." with a subtitle line; at banner-right a primary button "Enter waiting room".

## Screen: dashboard
- Header: logged-in; "Dashboard" pill active.
- Main content: vertical stack of bordered panels, each with an accent top bar.
  1. Profile panel (top): at panel-left a large circular avatar; to its right "admin" (bold) over "admin@example.com". Below that, sub-heading "Choose your avatar" with a horizontal row of 6 circular avatar swatches (first one shown with a selected outline).
  2. Two-column row:
     - Left panel: "Progress summary" heading. Inside, a 4-column row of stat tiles (Total points, Words learned, Flashcards reviewed, Crosswords solved — each shows "0" big over a label). Below it a 3-column row of tiles (Unjumbles solved, Syn/Ant correct, Fill-blanks correct). Then a line "This week (Eloquence & Expression) — 0 of 10 words touched" with a thin progress bar beneath. Bottom of panel: secondary button "Reset progress".
     - Right panel: "Weekly word set" heading at panel-top with "‹ Prev" button at panel-top-left of the heading row and "Next ›" button at panel-top-right; centered subheading "Week 1 — Eloquence & Expression". Below, an ordered list (1–10) of the ten words with bold word and "— definition" text.
  3. "Recent activity" panel: line "No activity yet — try a flashcard session." with "flashcard" as a link.
  4. "Jump into an activity" panel: a horizontal row of 6 buttons — Flashcards, Crossword, Unjumble, Syn/Ant, Fill blank (primary), Waiting room (secondary).
