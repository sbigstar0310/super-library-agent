# Layout spec

## Screen: browse
- Header: top of viewport, full width, dark-red bar — "Crypto Escrow" wordmark at header-left; nav button group at header-center with three buttons in a row: "Browse" (active, light fill), "My Products", "Transactions"; at header-right the text "admin (both)" followed by a "Logout" button.
- Main content: below the header on a light-gray background. Page heading "Available Cryptocurrencies" at top-left of the main area.
- Below the heading: a row of product cards aligned to main-left, two visible side by side with dark-red borders.
  - First card (left): title "Bitcoin (BTC)" at card-top; price "$65,000 / unit" directly below; "Seller: seller1" and "Stock: 0.5" stacked beneath; description text "Premium BTC at market rate."; "Buy" primary button at card-bottom-left.
  - Second card (right of first): title "Ethereum (ETH)" at card-top; "$3,200 / unit"; "Seller: seller1"; "Stock: 2"; description "ETH available for instant transfer."; "Buy" primary button at card-bottom-left.
- Interactive elements: header nav buttons, "Logout" at header-right, "Buy" inside each card.

## Screen: buy-form
- Header: identical to browse screen — wordmark at header-left, nav buttons center, "admin (both)" and "Logout" at header-right.
- Main content: same page as browse with heading "Available Cryptocurrencies" at top-left and the same two product cards in a row.
- Difference from browse: the first card (Bitcoin) is expanded to show an inline buy form occupying the lower half of the card.
  - Form (inside Bitcoin card, below the description): label "Quantity" → numeric input (with up/down spinners, prefilled with "1") spanning the card width; below the input a row of two buttons at card-bottom-left: "Confirm Buy" (primary, dark-red fill) and "Cancel" (secondary, dark fill).
- Second card (Ethereum) unchanged with its "Buy" button at card-bottom-left.

## Screen: dispute-form
- Header: identical layout — wordmark left, nav buttons center ("Transactions" highlighted as active), "admin (both)" and "Logout" at header-right.
- Main content: page heading "Transactions" at top-left.
- Below heading: a full-width table with a dark-red header row. Columns left-to-right: "#", "Product", "Qty", "Total", "Buyer", "Seller", "Status", "Actions".
  - Row 1: 1 | Bitcoin (BTC) | 0.1 BTC | $6,500 | admin | seller1 | yellow "pending" pill | "Deposit to Escrow" primary button.
  - Row 2: 2 | Ethereum (ETH) | 1 ETH | $3,200 | admin | seller1 | blue "deposited" pill | two buttons in a row: "Confirm Receipt" (primary) and "Open Dispute" (secondary dark).
  - Row 3: 3 | Bitcoin (BTC) | 0.05 BTC | $3,250 | admin | seller1 | red "disputed" pill | two buttons: "Resolve: Release" (primary) and "Resolve: Refund" (secondary dark).
- Below the table: a bordered panel spanning the main content width.
  - Panel heading "Open dispute for tx #2" at panel-top-left.
  - Form inside panel: label "Reason" → multi-line textarea (full panel width); button row at panel-bottom-left: "Submit Dispute" (primary dark-red) and "Cancel" (secondary dark).

## Screen: login
- No top header. Page background light gray.
- Main content: a single centered card (top-center of viewport with vertical offset) with a dark-red border.
  - Card-top-left: wordmark "Crypto Escrow".
  - Below wordmark: a row of two equal-width tab buttons spanning card width — "Login" (active, dark-red fill, left) and "Register" (inactive, light-gray fill, right).
  - Form (stacked, full card width):
    - Label "Username or Email" → single-line text input.
    - Label "Password" → single-line password input.
  - "Login" primary button at card-bottom-left.

## Screen: my-products
- Header: identical layout — wordmark left, nav buttons center ("My Products" highlighted as active), "admin (both)" and "Logout" at header-right.
- Main content: page heading "My Products" at top-left.
- Below heading: a bordered panel spanning the main content width.
  - Panel heading "Add new product" at panel-top-left.
  - Form inside panel using a two-column grid:
    - Row 1: left column — label "Name" → text input; right column — label "Symbol" → text input.
    - Row 2: left column — label "Price (USD)" → text input; right column — label "Quantity" → text input (narrower).
    - Row 3 (spans full width): label "Description" → multi-line textarea.
  - "Add Product" primary button at panel-bottom-left below the form.
- Below the add-product panel: a full-width table with a dark-red header row. Columns: "Name", "Symbol", "Price", "Stock", "Action".
  - Row 1: Solana (SOL) | SOL | $150 | 20 | "Delete" secondary dark button at row-right.

## Screen: register
- No top header. Page background light gray.
- Main content: a single centered card (top-center of viewport with vertical offset) with a dark-red border.
  - Card-top-left: wordmark "Crypto Escrow".
  - Below wordmark: row of two equal-width tab buttons — "Login" (inactive, light-gray fill) and "Register" (active, dark-red fill).
  - Form (stacked, full card width):
    - Label "Username" → text input.
    - Label "Email" → text input.
    - Label "Password" → password input.
    - Label "Role" → select dropdown, prefilled value "Both".
  - "Register" primary button at card-bottom-left.
