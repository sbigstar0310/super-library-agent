# Layout spec

## Screen: chat
- Header (top of viewport, full width, chocolate bar): "♥ Matrimonial" wordmark at header-left; at header-right, in a single row: italic "Hi, Aisha" greeting, then four buttons in order — "Search", "Messages", "My Profile", "Logout".
- Main content (center, single rounded chocolate panel spanning most of the viewport width, top-anchored just below the header):
  - Panel title "Messages" at panel-top-left, with a thin horizontal divider directly beneath spanning panel width.
  - Below the divider, the panel is split into two columns:
    - Left column (middle-left of panel, narrower): empty-state text "No conversations yet. Visit a profile and click \"Send Message\"." at column-top-left.
    - Right column (middle-right of panel, wider, conversation viewer area): placeholder text "Select a conversation." at column-top-left.
  - The remainder of the panel below these texts is empty.

  - Form (card-middle): two fields in a single row — label "Email" above an input at card-middle-left half, label "Password" above an input at card-middle-right half.
  - Below the inputs, a row of two buttons at card-middle-left: primary "Login" (light pill), then secondary "Create Account" (dark/chocolate pill).
