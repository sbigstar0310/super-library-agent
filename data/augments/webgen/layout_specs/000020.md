# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport, full width — wordmark "TelcoOne" at header-left; nav buttons in a row at header-right in order: Home (highlighted/active), Plans, Support, Account, Logout (admin).
- Main content: below header, centered column.
  - Top region: large hero card spanning page width (page-top). Inside the card: heading "Welcome to TelcoOne" at card-top-center; subtitle "Reliable service plans and 24/7 customer support — built around you." centered below the heading; a row of two buttons centered at card-bottom — "Browse Plans" (primary CTA) on the left, "Get Help" (secondary CTA) on the right.
  - Middle region: a row of three equal-width feature cards directly below the hero (page-middle). Each card has a bold title at card-top-left and a short description below it:
    - First card: "Nationwide Coverage" — "Talk, text, and stream on our high-speed network."
    - Second card: "Flexible Plans" — "From pay-as-you-go to unlimited family bundles."
    - Third card: "Live Support" — "Chat with our team any time, day or night."
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width — text "© 2026 TelcoOne — Stay Connected" centered.

## Screen: logout-home
- Identical visible layout to the `home` screen: same header, hero card with "Welcome to TelcoOne" and the two CTAs ("Browse Plans", "Get Help"), same three feature cards row, same footer. Header nav shows the same items including "Logout (admin)".

## Screen: plans
- Header: same as home — wordmark at header-left; nav at header-right with Plans highlighted/active.
- Main content: page-top-left heading "Service Plans"; subtitle "Choose the plan that fits how you connect." directly below.
  - Plan grid: a single row of four equal-width plan cards spanning page width below the subtitle. Each card has, top-to-bottom and left-aligned:
    - Plan name (card-top-left).
    - Price with "/mo" suffix on the same line below the name.
    - Three label/value lines: "Data:" <value>, "Minutes:" <value>, "Texts:" <value>.
    - A row of two buttons at card-bottom-left: "Details" (secondary) and "Subscribe" (secondary), side by side.
  - Card contents:
    - First card: "Basic Talk" — $15/mo — Data: 1 GB, Minutes: 500 min, Texts: Unlimited.
    - Second card: "Standard Connect" — $30/mo — Data: 10 GB, Minutes: Unlimited, Texts: Unlimited.
    - Third card: "Gold Unlimited" — $55/mo — Data: Unlimited, Minutes: Unlimited, Texts: Unlimited.
    - Fourth card: "Family Share" — $90/mo — Data: 50 GB shared, Minutes: Unlimited, Texts: Unlimited.
- Footer: bottom-center — "© 2026 TelcoOne — Stay Connected".

## Screen: plan-details
- Header: same as plans, with Plans highlighted.
- Main content: identical "Service Plans" heading, subtitle, and four-card plan grid as the `plans` screen. The first card's "Details" button appears pressed/active.
  - Details panel: below the plan grid, a full-width card (page-middle).
    - Title at card-top-left: "Basic Talk — Details".
    - Below the title, left-aligned rows:
      - "Monthly price: $15".
      - A single line with three inline label/value pairs separated by middots: "Data: 1 GB · Minutes: 500 min · Texts: Unlimited".
      - Heading "Included features:" followed by a bulleted list: "Nationwide calling", "Voicemail", "Caller ID".
    - "Close" button at card-bottom-left.
- Footer: bottom-center — "© 2026 TelcoOne — Stay Connected".

## Screen: plan-subscribed
- Header: same as plans, with Plans highlighted.
- Main content: heading "Service Plans" and subtitle at page-top-left.
  - Notification banner: full-width banner directly below the subtitle containing the message "You are now subscribed to Basic Talk." (text left-aligned within the banner).
  - Plan grid: same four-card row as on the `plans` screen, positioned below the banner.
- Footer: bottom-center — "© 2026 TelcoOne — Stay Connected".

## Screen: support
- Header: same nav, Support highlighted/active.
- Main content: heading "Customer Support" at page-top-left.
  - Below the heading, a two-column row of equal-width panels:
    - Left panel ("Frequently Asked"): title at panel-top-left; below it a vertical stack of four collapsed FAQ buttons, each full panel width, left-aligned text prefixed with a "+" indicator:
      - "+ How do I change my plan?"
      - "+ How do I pay my bill?"
      - "+ Why is my signal weak?"
      - "+ How do I add a new line?"
    - Right panel ("Live Chat"): title at panel-top-left; below it a chat transcript area containing one message bubble at the top-left of the area with a small "bot" label above the text "Welcome to TelcoOne Support. How can we help?"; below the transcript area a message composer row — a text input with placeholder "Type a message…" stretching most of the width and a "Send" button at the row's right end.
- Footer: bottom-center — "© 2026 TelcoOne — Stay Connected".

## Screen: support-faq-open
- Identical to the `support` screen, except the first FAQ item is expanded:
  - The first FAQ button shows "– How do I change my plan?" (minus indicator).
  - Directly below that button, an answer panel of the same width displays: "Go to the Plans page and click Subscribe on the plan you want."
  - The remaining three FAQ items ("+ How do I pay my bill?", "+ Why is my signal weak?", "+ How do I add a new line?") remain collapsed below.
- Right "Live Chat" panel and footer unchanged.

## Screen: account-edit
