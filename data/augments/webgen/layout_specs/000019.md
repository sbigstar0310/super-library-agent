# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full width): wordmark "▲ BrickWorks" at header-left; nav row at header-center-left with buttons "Home" (active/highlighted), "Solutions", "News & Trends", "Customer Stories", "Contact"; search input "Search products, articles…" and "Search" button at header-right.
- Main content (below header, stacked vertically, page-center column with side padding):
  - Hero banner (top of main, full-width filled block): heading "Software & staffing built for growing businesses" at banner-top-left; supporting paragraph "BrickWorks helps small and medium-sized businesses pick the right tools, hire the right people, and ship the right software — without the enterprise overhead." below it; two buttons in a row at banner-bottom-left: "Explore Solutions" (primary), "Talk to Us" (secondary).
  - Section "Featured Solutions" (below hero): section heading at section-top-left; three outlined cards in a single row below. Each card: product name at card-top-left with a "Product" badge inline to its right; description text below (e.g., "A simple, affordable CRM for small teams.", "Lightweight accounting & invoicing.", "Helpdesk software for growing teams."). Cards: BrickCRM, BrickBooks, BrickDesk.
  - Below the cards row, a filled "See all solutions" button at section-bottom-left.
  - Section "Latest from our blog" (below): heading at section-top-left; one outlined article card spanning full width: article title "Why SMBs Are Adopting AI Faster Than Ever" at card-top-left, date "2026-04-12" below, paragraph excerpt below, then filled "Read industry news" button at card-bottom-left.
  - Section "Customer success" (below): heading at section-top-left; one outlined case card full width: title "Maple & Co. Bakery" at card-top-left, metadata line "Retail / Food · 38% faster order turnaround" below, paragraph below, filled "More success stories" button at card-bottom-left.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full-width filled bar): "© 2026 BrickWorks Software Solutions" at footer-left; tagline "Software & staffing for growing businesses." at footer-right.
