# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport — wordmark "DMV Practice Test" at header-left (top-left). Below the wordmark, a horizontal nav row at header-bottom-left containing four pill/box buttons in this order: "Home" (active/filled), "Practice Questions", "Simulated Exam", "Error Analysis". A horizontal divider line runs across the bottom edge of the header.
- Main content: top-left of the area below the header.
  - Top: section heading "Welcome".
  - Below heading: a single intro paragraph — "Prepare for your driver's license exam with 10 practice questions, a simulated exam, and error analysis."
  - Below the paragraph: a bulleted list of three items, each on its own line:
    - "Practice Questions: review answers one at a time."
    - "Simulated Exam: take a timed-style test, then see your score."
    - "Error Analysis: review mistakes from past exams."
- Interactive elements: the four header nav buttons (Home active).
- Empty state / loading text: none.

## Screen: practice
- Header: top of viewport — wordmark "DMV Practice Test" at header-left. Nav row at header-bottom-left with four buttons in order: "Home", "Practice Questions" (active/filled), "Simulated Exam", "Error Analysis". Horizontal divider at header-bottom.
- Main content: top-left of the area below the header, stacked vertically.
  - Top: section heading "Practice Questions".
  - Middle-top: a horizontal row of ten circular pager buttons numbered "1" through "10", left-aligned. Button "1" is filled (active state); buttons 2–10 are outlined.
  - Middle: a bordered rectangular card occupying roughly the left two-thirds of the content width.
    - Card-top: small label "Question 1 of 10".
    - Below label: question text "What does a red octagonal sign mean?".
    - Below question: four answer options stacked vertically, each rendered as a full-width outlined rectangular row (clickable option):
      - "A. Yield"
      - "B. Stop"
      - "C. Slow down"
      - "D. No entry"
    - Card-bottom: a row of two buttons at card-bottom-left — "Check Answer" (appears disabled/muted) and "Next" (filled/primary), in that order.
- Forms: none (multiple-choice selectable rows).
- Interactive elements: four header nav buttons; ten numbered pager buttons; four answer-option rows; "Check Answer" button; "Next" button.
- Empty state / loading text: none.

## Screen: exam
- Header: top of viewport — wordmark "DMV Practice Test" at header-left. Nav row at header-bottom-left with four buttons in order: "Home", "Practice Questions", "Simulated Exam" (active/filled), "Error Analysis". Horizontal divider at header-bottom.
- Main content: top-left of the area below the header, stacked vertically.
  - Top: section heading "Simulated Exam".
  - Below heading: a single paragraph — "The exam contains 10 questions. Answer them all, then submit to see your score."
  - Below the paragraph: a single primary button "Start Exam" at left.
- Interactive elements: four header nav buttons; "Start Exam" button.
- Empty state / loading text: none.

## Screen: analysis
- Header: top of viewport — wordmark "DMV Practice Test" at header-left. Nav row at header-bottom-left with four buttons in order: "Home", "Practice Questions", "Simulated Exam", "Error Analysis" (active/filled). Horizontal divider at header-bottom.
- Main content: top-left of the area below the header, stacked vertically.
  - Top: section heading "Error Analysis".
  - Below heading: empty-state text "No exam results yet. Take a simulated exam to see analysis here."
- Interactive elements: four header nav buttons.
- Empty state / loading text: visible empty-state line below the heading (text above), top-left of main region.
