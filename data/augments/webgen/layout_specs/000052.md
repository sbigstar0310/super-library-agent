# Layout spec

## Screen: jobs
- Header: top of viewport, spanning full width — at header-left, a small hammers/tools icon followed by the wordmark "BlueCollar Jobs"; at header-right, two buttons in a row: "Browse Jobs" (filled primary) and "My Profile" (outlined).
- Main content: below the header, centered horizontally with side margins.
  - Top-left of main: page title "Find Your Next Job" (large heading) with a subtitle directly below it reading "Browse openings for construction workers, plumbers, electricians, and more."
  - Below the title block (top-center of main): a full-width search input with placeholder "Search by title, company, or location…".
  - Below the search input (middle-top of main): a horizontal row of category filter pills, left-aligned, in order: "All" (selected/filled), "Construction", "Plumbing", "Electrical", "HVAC", "Welding".
  - Below the filters (middle of main): a grid of job cards in three columns. Visible rows:
    - Row 1: "Construction Worker" / "Journeyman Plumber" / "Residential Electrician".
    - Row 2: "HVAC Technician" / "Carpenter" / "Welder (MIG/TIG)".
    - Row 3: "Apprentice Electrician" / "Heavy Equipment Operator" / (third cell empty).
  - Each job card layout: at card-top-left, the job title (bold); at card-top-right, the pay range (e.g. "$22 - $28/hr") in accent color. Directly below the title (card-upper-middle), a meta row with company name, a bullet separator, location, another bullet separator, and a category tag in accent color (e.g. "Ridgeline Builders • Denver, CO • Construction"). In card-middle, a short description paragraph (2 lines). At card-bottom-left, a filled primary button "View & Apply".
- Footer: bottom of viewport, centered text "Connecting skilled workers with employers who need them."

## Screen: job-detail-modal
- Underlying page: same as `jobs` screen, dimmed.
- Modal: anchored at viewport-center, a centered card overlay.
  - Modal-top-left: job title "Construction Worker" (large heading).
  - Directly below title (modal-upper): subtitle "Ridgeline Builders — Denver, CO".
  - Modal-middle, stacked as labeled rows:
    - "Pay:" followed by "$22 - $28/hr".
    - "Category:" followed by "Construction".
    - A descriptive paragraph: "Framing and concrete work on residential builds. OSHA-10 preferred."
  - Modal-bottom-left: two buttons in a row — "Submit Application" (filled primary) and "Close" (outlined secondary).

## Screen: profile-edit
- Header: identical to other screens — wordmark at header-left, "Browse Jobs" (outlined) and "My Profile" (filled primary) at header-right.
- Main content: top-left of main, indented from the page edge.
  - Top: page title "Edit Profile" with subtitle directly below: "Tell employers who you are and what trades you work."
  - Form (middle of main, single column, left-aligned, roughly half page width): each field has its label on its own line above the input.
    - "Full Name" → single-line text input (prefilled "Jane Welder").
    - "Email Address" → single-line text input (prefilled "jane@example.com").
    - "Phone" → single-line text input (prefilled "555-0100").
    - "Skills (comma separated)" → single-line text input (prefilled "Welding, Framing").
    - "Qualifications & Certifications" → multi-line textarea (prefilled "OSHA-10, 5 yrs experience").
    - "Photo" → file picker showing a "Choose File" button next to "No file chosen" text.
  - Form-bottom-left: two buttons in a row — "Save Profile" (filled primary) and "Cancel" (outlined secondary).
- Footer: bottom-center text "Connecting skilled workers with employers who need them."

## Screen: profile-view
- Header: identical to other screens — wordmark at header-left; at header-right, "Browse Jobs" (outlined) and "My Profile" (filled primary).
- Main content: top-left of main below header.
  - Top-left: page title "My Profile".
  - Below the title (middle-top of main): a full-width bordered profile card.
    - Card-left: a square photo placeholder containing centered text "No Photo".
    - Card-right of the photo, top-aligned: user name "Jane Welder" (large accent-colored heading).
    - Directly below the name (card-middle-right), a vertical list of labeled facts (each label bold):
      - "Email:" jane@example.com
      - "Phone:" 555-0100
      - "Skills:" Welding, Framing
      - "Qualifications:" OSHA-10, 5 yrs experience
    - Card-bottom-left of the right column: filled primary button "Edit Profile".
- Footer: bottom-center text "Connecting skilled workers with employers who need them."
