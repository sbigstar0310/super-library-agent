# Layout spec

Global chrome (present on every screen):
- Header: top of viewport, full-width slate-gray bar — wordmark "Forum" at header-left; nav buttons in a row at header-right.
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full-width slate-gray bar — text "© 2026 Forum" at footer-center.
- Page background: ghost-white. Main content sits in a single slate-gray rounded card horizontally centered below the header.

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport — "Forum" at header-left; at header-right, a row of four outlined buttons in order: Home, New Post, admin, Logout.
- Main content: page-center, single rounded slate-gray card spanning most of the viewport width.
  - Card-top: heading "Browse Posts" at card-top-left.
  - Below heading: a row with a wide "Search posts..." text input at row-left taking most of the width, and an "All Categories" dropdown select at row-right.
  - Below the search row: a horizontal row of pill filter buttons, left-aligned, in order: All (active/filled), General, Technology, Gaming, Music, Sports.
  - Below filters: a vertical stack of post cards (rounded, lighter slate panels), each card:
    - Card-top-left: post title in bold.
    - Below title: a meta row left-aligned with a category pill (e.g., "General"), then text "by admin", then "N replies", then a date.
    - Card-bottom: a one-line excerpt of the post body.
  - Three post cards visible: "Welcome to the Forum!" (General, by admin, 1 replies, 5/1/2026, excerpt "This is the very first post. Feel free to introduce yourself."); "Best frameworks in 2026?" (Technology, by admin, 0 replies, 5/2/2026, excerpt "What is everyone using these days? React still rules for me."); "Favorite indie game right now" (Gaming, by admin, 0 replies, 5/3/2026, excerpt "Share your current obsession.").
- Footer: bottom of viewport — "© 2026 Forum" at footer-center.

## Screen: login
- Header: top of viewport — "Forum" at header-left; nav buttons at header-right in order: Home, Login, Register.
- Main content: page-center (upper middle), a narrower rounded slate-gray card.
  - Card-top-left: heading "Login".
  - Form (stacked, left-aligned labels above full-width inputs):
    - Username or email → text input → (no submit on this row)
