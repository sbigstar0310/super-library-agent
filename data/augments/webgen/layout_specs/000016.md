# Layout spec

Global background is lemon chiffon; all panels/cards/header/footer use a chocolate (warm orange/brown) fill with rounded corners. Header and footer are shared across all screens.

- Shared Header (top of viewport, full width panel): wordmark group at header-left consisting of a small sun-burst glyph followed by "SunPeak Solar"; navigation at header-right as a horizontal row of pill buttons in order: Home, About, Products, News, Contact. The button matching the current screen is rendered in an inverted (light-fill) style to indicate the active tab.
- Shared Footer (bottom of viewport, full width panel): centered single line of text "© 2026 SunPeak Solar — Powering tomorrow with the sun."

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport — as described in Shared Header. "Home" is the active nav button.
- Main content: center column, stacked top-to-bottom as two large rounded panels.
  - First panel (top, hero): contents center-aligned. Heading at panel-top-center "Clean Energy for a Brighter Tomorrow". Below it, a paragraph at panel-middle-center: "SunPeak Solar designs, manufactures, and installs high-efficiency photovoltaic systems for homes, businesses, and utility-scale projects across the globe." At panel-bottom-center, a row of two buttons side-by-side: primary CTA "Explore Products" (light fill) on the left and secondary CTA "Get in Touch" (outline/link style on dark) on the right.
  - Second panel (below hero, "Why SunPeak"): section title "Why SunPeak" at panel-top-left, followed by a horizontal rule. Below the rule, a 3-column row of equal-width stat cards. Each card has a bold metric at card-top-left and a description sentence at card-middle-left.
    - Column 1: "20+ Years" / "Of solar engineering and manufacturing excellence."
    - Column 2: "500 MW" / "Installed capacity worldwide, and growing every quarter."
    - Column 3: "98%" / "Customer satisfaction rating across residential installs."
- Footer: bottom of viewport — as described in Shared Footer.
