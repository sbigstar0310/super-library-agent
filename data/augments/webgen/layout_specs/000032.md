# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: full-width bar at top of viewport — at header-left a square "TV" tile followed by the wordmark "TruthVoice"; immediately right of the wordmark a primary nav row with labels "Home" (active/highlighted), "Report", "Forum", "Search", "News & Blog"; at header-right a pill-shaped "@admin" badge followed by a "Log out" button.
- Main content: page-center column.
  - Top region: a large dark hero panel spanning the content width. Inside the hero: at hero-top-left a heading "Expose the truths that matter."; below it a two-line paragraph "TruthVoice is a secure platform for whistleblowers, journalists, and citizens to share insight, evidence, and ideas about the systems that shape our lives."; below the paragraph a row of two buttons at hero-middle-left — primary "Submit anonymously" then secondary "Join the discussion".
  - Middle region: a 4-column row of feature cards. From left to right each card has a bold title and short description: "Anonymous Reports" / "Submit insights and evidence with no account required and no tracking."; "Open Discussion" / "Debate context and meaning with a community of truth-seekers."; "Search Everything" / "Find articles, threads, and evidence across the platform."; "News & Analysis" / "Read original reporting based on community-sourced material."
  - Bottom region: section heading "Latest stories" at region-top-left, followed by a 3-column row of story cards. Each card top-left shows a pill-shaped category badge (BUSINESS, SOCIETY, HEALTH), then a bold headline, then a one-line summary, then a date in small muted text at card-bottom-left. Headlines: "Leaked Memos Reveal Corporate Cover-Up at MegaCorp" (2026-05-18); "How a City Hall Insider Exposed Procurement Fraud" (2026-05-12); "Big Pharma Trial Data: What the Public Was Not Told" (2026-05-05).
- Footer: full-width dark bar at bottom-center of viewport containing the text "TruthVoice © 2026 — Speak truth. Stay anonymous."

## Screen: article
- Header: same as home — TV tile + wordmark at header-left, nav row centered-left, "@admin" badge and "Log out" at header-right.
- Main content: left-aligned column starting page-top-left.
  - Top: a row with a bordered "← Back to news" button at row-left followed by a "BUSINESS" pill badge.
  - Below: large bold headline "Leaked Memos Reveal Corporate Cover-Up at MegaCorp" wrapping over two lines.
  - Below headline: a small muted date "2026-05-18".
  - Below date: an italic summary line "Internal documents show executives knew about safety hazards for years."
  - Below summary: a body paragraph of plain text describing the leak and authenticity confirmation.
- Footer: full-width dark bar at viewport-bottom with "TruthVoice © 2026 — Speak truth. Stay anonymous."

## Screen: forum-new-thread
- Header: same as home — "Forum" nav item is highlighted (active).
- Main content: page-center column.
  - Top row: heading "Discussion Forum" at row-left and a "Cancel" button at row-right.
  - Below: a bordered card containing the new-thread form, stacked vertically:
    - Single-line text input with placeholder "Thread title".
    - Multi-line textarea with placeholder "What is on your mind?".
    - Full-width primary button labeled "Post thread" at card-bottom.
  - Below the form card: a stacked list of existing thread cards (full-width). Each card shows a bold thread title at card-top-left, then a meta row of "by @user", date, "N replies", then a one-line preview of the thread body. Visible threads: "Best practices for protecting your identity online" by @anon_owl, 2026-05-20, 2 replies — "What VPN, OS, and messaging setup do you recommend before submitting evidence?"; "How should journalists verify leaked corporate documents?" by @press_watch, 2026-05-15, 0 replies — "Curious about cross-referencing techniques the community uses."
- Footer: full-width dark bar at viewport-bottom.
