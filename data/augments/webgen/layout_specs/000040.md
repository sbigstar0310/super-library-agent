# Layout spec

Shared across all screens:
- Header (top of viewport, full width): "Apex Leadership Consulting" wordmark at header-left; horizontal row of nav buttons at header-right in order: Home, Workshops, Classes, Podcasts, Blog, Cart. The button corresponding to the current screen has an inverted/lighter pill style versus the others.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full width): single line of centered text "© 2026 Apex Leadership Consulting · Building stronger leaders since 2008".
- Main content sits between header and footer, horizontally centered with side margins.

## Screen: home
- Header: as shared; Home nav button shown as active (light/inverted pill).
- Main content: top-center — a large rounded hero card spanning the content width. Inside the hero, content is vertically stacked and centered: title "Develop the Leaders Your Business Deserves" at card-top-center; below it a two-line descriptive paragraph "Apex partners with growing companies to deliver hands-on leadership training, strategic workshops, and online learning that move the needle."; at card-bottom-center, a row of two primary buttons side by side: "Explore Workshops" (left) and "Browse Online Classes" (right).
- Below the hero: a three-column row of equal-width rounded cards.
  - First column (left): heading "Workshops" at card-top-left, body text "Immersive in-person and virtual sessions led by senior consultants." in the middle, link "View workshops →" at card-bottom-left.
  - Second column (center): heading "Online Classes", body "Self-paced certificate programs your whole team can take together.", link "View classes →" at card-bottom-left.
  - Third column (right): heading "Podcasts & Blog", body "Weekly insights from practitioners who've sat in the seat.", link "Listen now →" at card-bottom-left.
- Empty space fills below the cards down to the footer.
