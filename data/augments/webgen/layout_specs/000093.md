# Layout spec

## Screen: showcase
- Header (top of viewport, full-width bar): "World of Imagination" wordmark at header-left; at header-right a row of nav buttons in order: "Showcase" (current, visually emphasized), "Share Ideas", "Discussions", then the text "Hi, admin", then a "Logout" button.
- Main content (below header, page-center column):
  - Top: page title "Creative Showcase" at top-left of main.
  - Below title: subtitle text "Browse imaginative works shared by the community." at left.
  - Middle: a single row grid of three equal-width bordered cards.
    - Card 1 (first column): title "Floating City" at card-top-left; description "A metropolis suspended by giant balloons above the clouds." below title; a thin horizontal divider; footer row with "by skywalker" at card-bottom-left and a heart badge "♥ 12" at card-bottom-right.
    - Card 2 (second column): title "Crystal Forest"; description "Trees made of sparkling crystal that sing at dawn."; divider; footer "by dreamer" at left and badge "♥ 8" at right.
    - Card 3 (third column): title "Time Garden"; description "A garden where each flower blooms in a different century."; divider; footer "by admin" at left and badge "♥ 20" at right.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full-width): centered text "World of Imagination © 2026".

## Screen: share-ideas
- Header (top of viewport): same as showcase — wordmark at header-left; header-right row: "Showcase", "Share Ideas" (current, emphasized), "Discussions", "Hi, admin", "Logout".
- Main content (page-center column):
  - Top: page title "Share Your Idea" at top-left.
  - Below title: subtitle "Add a spark to the world's imagination." at left.
  - Middle: a full-width bordered card containing the form.
    - Form (inside card, stacked top-to-bottom, left-aligned):
      - Label "Title" → single-line text input with placeholder "A short title".
      - Label "Description" → multi-line textarea with placeholder "Describe your imaginative idea…".
      - Submit button labeled "Share Idea" at card-bottom-left (primary CTA).
- Footer (bottom of viewport): centered "World of Imagination © 2026".

## Screen: share-ideas-error
- Header: same as share-ideas.
- Main content: identical layout to share-ideas with an added error banner:
  - Title input (empty, placeholder "A short title").
  - Description textarea (empty, placeholder "Describe your imaginative idea…").
  - Full-width error banner below the textarea, containing text "Please provide both a title and description." (styled as a filled primary-tone bar spanning the form width).
  - "Share Idea" submit button below the error banner, at card-bottom-left.
- Footer: centered "World of Imagination © 2026".

## Screen: discussions
- Header (top of viewport): wordmark at header-left; header-right row: "Showcase", "Share Ideas", "Discussions" (current, emphasized), "Hi, admin", "Logout".
- Main content (page-center column):
  - Top: page title "Discussions" at top-left.
  - Below title: subtitle "Start a topic or join the conversation." at left.
  - New-topic row (full width): single-line text input with placeholder "Start a new topic…" occupying most of the row width at left, and "Post Topic" button at the row's right end.
  - Below new-topic row: stacked list of bordered topic cards, one per row.
    - Topic card 1 (full-width bordered card):
      - Card-top: bold title "What sparks your imagination?" then italic subtitle "started by admin" beneath it, both left-aligned.
      - Thin horizontal divider.
      - Reply entry: "dreamer:" (bold) followed by "Long walks at twilight — the sky is a canvas." at left.
      - Card-bottom row: reply input with placeholder "Write a reply…" occupying most width at left, and "Reply" button at the row's right end.
    - Topic card 2 (full-width bordered card):
      - Card-top: bold title "Favorite fictional worlds?" then italic subtitle "started by skywalker".
      - Divider.
      - Italic empty-state text "No replies yet." at left.
      - Card-bottom row: reply input placeholder "Write a reply…" at left and "Reply" button at right.
- Footer (bottom of viewport): centered "World of Imagination © 2026".
