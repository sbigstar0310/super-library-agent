# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full-width teal bar): wordmark "Fishing Contest Hub" at header-left; primary nav buttons in a row at header-center — "Home", "Announcements", "Results", "Photo Gallery", "Register"; at header-right "Hi, admin" text followed by a "Logout" button.
- Main content (below header, centered column):
  - Top: hero card (full-width of content column) — heading "Welcome to the Fishing Contest Hub" at card-top-left; one-line paragraph "Browse upcoming contests, register to compete, check past results, and view photos from our community of anglers." below the heading; two buttons in a row at card-bottom-left — primary "See Announcements" and secondary "Register Now".
  - Middle: section "Upcoming Contests" heading at section-top-left, with an underline rule below. Two contest cards in a row (two-column grid):
    - First card (left): title "Spring Bass Open 2026" at card-top-left; meta line "2026-06-12 · Lake Serene" below; description paragraph below.
    - Second card (right): title "Summer Trout Challenge"; meta line "2026-07-20 · Pinecrest River"; description paragraph below.
  - Bottom: section "Latest Results" heading at section-top-left, with underline rule. One result card spanning full width:
    - Title "Winter Ice Classic 2026" at card-top-left, date "2026-02-08" below.
    - Ordered list (1, 2, 3): "Marcus Lin — Northern Pike (6.4 kg)", "Ava Brooks — Walleye (4.1 kg)", "Diego Ruiz — Perch (3.2 kg)".
- Footer (bottom-center of viewport): "© 2026 Fishing Contest Hub".
