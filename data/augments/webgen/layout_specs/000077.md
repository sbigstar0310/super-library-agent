# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: full-width band at top of viewport — wordmark "EngTools" at header-left; at header-right a row with "Log in" link (underlined text) then a "Register" button (outlined button), in that order.
- Main content: page-center, stacking top to bottom:
  - Page title "Tools for Engineers" at top-center, with a one-line subtitle "Discover, compare, and review the tools that power your stack." directly below it, also top-center.
  - Search bar row below the title (page-center): a wide text input with placeholder "Search tools…" occupying most of the row at row-left/center, and a narrow dropdown showing "All" at row-right.
  - Tools grid below the search row, page-center: 3 columns × 3 rows of bordered cards (last row has only 2 cards at row-left and row-center; row-right slot is empty).
    - Each card layout:
      - Card-top-left: tool name (bold, prominent). Card-top-right: a pill-shaped category badge (e.g., "Version Control", "DevOps", "Editor", "API", "CI/CD", "Design").
      - Card-middle: short description paragraph (2 lines).
      - Card-bottom row: price/license label at card-bottom-left (bold), and a 5-star rating glyph with "No ratings (0)" text at card-bottom-right.
    - Cards in reading order: row1 = Git, Docker, VS Code; row2 = JetBrains IntelliJ, Postman, Jenkins; row3 = Kubernetes, Figma, (empty).
- Footer: full-width band at bottom of viewport — centered text "EngTools · For engineers, by engineers" at footer-center.

    - "Price:" label inline with value "Free / Open Source".
    - "Average rating:" label inline with a 5-star glyph and "— (0 reviews)".

  - Card-lower section, card-left:
    - "Reviews" heading.
    - "No reviews yet." text below.

- Footer: full-width band at bottom of viewport — centered text "EngTools · For engineers, by engineers".
