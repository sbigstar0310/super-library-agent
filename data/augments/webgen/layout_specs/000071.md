# Layout spec

## Screen: about
- Header (top of viewport, centered block above a horizontal divider): wordmark "Mira Hale" at header-top-center; tagline "Independent Visual & Brand Designer" directly below at header-center; a row of three pill-shaped nav buttons at header-bottom-center in this order — "About", "Portfolio", "Contact". On this screen the "About" pill appears as the active/selected affordance (filled), while "Portfolio" and "Contact" appear as outlined pills.
- Main content (below the header divider, centered column):
  - Top: section heading "About Me" at main-top-left of the content column.
  - Below heading: a paragraph of body text at main-upper-left reading: "I'm Mira, a visual and brand designer with nearly a decade of experience crafting identities that feel warm, considered, and built to last. I partner with small studios, restaurants, and independent publishers to translate their voice into a coherent visual language."
  - Middle: subsection heading "Experience" (with a small vertical accent bar at its left edge) at main-middle-left.
  - Directly below "Experience": a three-row table-like list, each row spanning the content column, separated by thin horizontal rules. Columns are arranged left → center → right:
    - Row 1: "2023–Present" (left), "Independent Designer" (center, bold), "Freelance Studio" (right).
    - Row 2: "2020–2023" (left), "Senior Brand Designer" (center, bold), "Linden & Co." (right).
    - Row 3: "2017–2020" (left), "Visual Designer" (center, bold), "Northwind Agency" (right).
  - Lower middle: subsection heading "Skills" (with a small vertical accent bar at its left edge) at main-lower-left.
  - Below "Skills": a single row of pill-shaped tags at main-lower-left, left-aligned, in this order: "Brand Identity", "Editorial Layout", "Illustration", "Packaging", "Type Design".
- Footer (bottom of viewport, above which is a thin horizontal divider): centered single line "© 2026 Mira Hale — Crafted with care." at footer-center.

## Screen: contact
- Header: same wordmark + tagline + three nav pills as other screens; "Contact" pill is active (filled), "About" and "Portfolio" outlined.
- Main content (centered column below header divider):
  - Top: section heading "Get in Touch" at main-top-left of the content column.
  - Directly below heading: intro line "Have a project in mind, or just want to say hello? I'd love to hear from you." at main-upper-left.
  - Upper-middle: a filled contact info card spanning the content column, containing three label/value rows aligned in two columns (label on the left, value on the right of the label):
    - Row 1: "Email" → "hello@mirahale.design" (rendered as a link).
    - Row 2: "Studio" → "Portland, Oregon".
    - Row 3: "Instagram" → "@mira.hale.design".
  - Lower portion: a vertical contact form, fields stacked top-to-bottom, each label sits above its input, all left-aligned within the content column:
    - "Name" → single-line text input.
    - "Email" → single-line text input.
    - "Message" → multi-line textarea (taller, with a resize handle at its bottom-right).
  - Below the form fields, at form-bottom-left: a primary filled button labeled "Send Message".
- Footer (bottom of viewport, above a thin divider): "© 2026 Mira Hale — Crafted with care." at footer-center.
