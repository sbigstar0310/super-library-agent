# Layout spec

## Screen: browse
- Header: top of viewport, full-width band — wordmark "SkillShare" at header-left; nav buttons in a row at header-right in order: "Browse" (filled/active), "Search", "My Profile", "Sign out (Ada)".
- Main content: below header, page-center column.
  - Title "Browse Members" at top-left of main, with a horizontal divider directly beneath spanning main width.
  - Member grid: three cards in a single row at top-center of main, equal width.
    - Card 1 (first column): name "Ada Lovelace" at card-top-left (bold), email "ada@example.com" directly below name; two skill pills "Mathematics" and "Algorithm Design" in a row at card-middle-left; certification pills "PhD Mathematics" then "Algorithm Design Certificate" stacked on next rows at card-bottom-left.
    - Card 2 (second column): name "Grace Hopper" at card-top-left, email "grace@example.com" directly below; skill pills "COBOL" and "Compiler Design" in a row at card-middle-left; certification pills "US Navy Rear Admiral" and "IEEE Fellow" in a row at card-bottom-left.
    - Card 3 (third column): name "Alan Turing" at card-top-left, email "alan@example.com" below; skill pills "Cryptography" and "Theoretical CS" in a row at card-middle-left; certification pill "PhD Princeton" at card-bottom-left.
- Interactive elements: header nav buttons at header-right. Skill pills use a lighter filled style; certification pills use a darker filled style.

## Screen: join
- Header: top of viewport, full-width band — wordmark "SkillShare" at header-left; nav buttons row at header-right: "Browse", "Search", "Sign in / Join".
- Main content: below header, page-center column.
  - Title "Join SkillShare" at top-left of main, horizontal divider directly beneath.
  - Bordered card spanning main width below title.
    - Mode toggle row at card-top-left: outlined "Sign in" button then filled "Create profile" button (Create profile is the active tab).
    - Stacked form below the toggle, each label above its input, left-aligned:
      - "Name" → single-line text input (full card width).
      - "Email" → single-line text input (full card width).
      - "Bio (optional)" → multi-line textarea (full card width).
    - Primary filled submit button "Create profile" at card-bottom-left.
- Forms: Name → text, Email → text, Bio (optional) → textarea, submit "Create profile".
