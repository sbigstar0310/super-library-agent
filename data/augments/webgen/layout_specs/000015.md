# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header: top of viewport, full width, cadet-blue band — wordmark "Riverside Clinical Office" at header-top-left; nav row directly below at header-bottom-left with four items "Home", "About Us", "Services", "Contact Us" (the "Home" item is active, indicated by an underline).
- Main content: center column below header, page background light cyan, three stacked cadet-blue rounded panels.
  - First panel (top): heading "Welcome to Riverside Clinical Office" at panel-top-left; below it a paragraph "We provide compassionate, evidence-based healthcare for the whole family. Our experienced clinicians are committed to helping you feel your best."
  - Second panel (middle): heading "Office Hours" at panel-top-left; below it three stacked rows (lighter cadet-blue rounded bars) each with left-aligned text:
    - "Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM"
    - "Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM"
    - "Sunday: Closed"
  - Third panel (bottom): heading "Get in Touch" at panel-top-center; paragraph "Have questions or want to book an appointment?" at panel-middle-center; a "Contact Us" button (light/white background, dark text) at panel-bottom-center.
- Interactive elements: nav links "Home", "About Us", "Services", "Contact Us" (header-bottom-left); "Contact Us" button (third panel, bottom-center).
- Footer: bottom of viewport, full width, cadet-blue band — centered text "© 2026 Riverside Clinical Office. All rights reserved."
