## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full width, dark bar): "TechHub" wordmark at header-left; immediately to its right a horizontal nav row with buttons "Home" (highlighted as active), "Articles", "Solutions", "Search", "Forum"; at header-right the text "Hi, admin" followed by a "Logout" button.
- Main content (below header, center column):
  - Hero banner (top, full-width blue rectangle): heading "TechHub" at banner-top-left; one line of body text below it ("Browse technical articles, search topics, and explore practical solutions."); a row of two buttons at banner-bottom-left — primary "Read articles" and secondary "Browse solutions".
  - Stats row (middle, three equal cards in one row): first card (left) shows large number "4" centered with label "Articles" below; middle card shows "3" with label "Solutions"; right card shows an infinity symbol "∞" with label "Discussions".
  - Recent articles panel (bottom, single full-width card): heading "Recent articles" at panel-top-left; below it a vertical list of three article rows separated by horizontal rules. Each row has a bold title on the first line and a subtitle line "<Category> · <YYYY-MM-DD>". Rows from top to bottom: "Understanding React 18 Concurrent Rendering" / "Frontend · 2026-03-12"; "Why Your Vite Build is Slow (and How to Fix It)" / "Tooling · 2026-02-22"; "A Practical Guide to PostgreSQL Indexes" / "Database · 2026-01-15".
- Footer (bottom of viewport, center-aligned): single line "TechHub · in-browser demo".
