# Layout spec

## Screen: home
- Header (top of viewport, full-width dark goldenrod bar): "Entertainment Directory" wordmark at header-left; nav buttons in a row at header-right — "Home", "Bands", "Advertisements", "Login", "Register".
- Main content (below header, left-aligned within page):
  - Top: heading "Welcome" at top-left.
  - Below heading: paragraph text "Discover bands, listen to samples, watch videos, and view advertisements."
  - Below paragraph: a row of two bordered stat cards aligned to the left. First card: large number "1" centered-top with label "Bands" below. Second card (right of first): large number "1" centered-top with label "Advertisements" below.
- Footer (bottom of viewport, full-width dark goldenrod bar): "© 2026 Entertainment Directory" text at footer-center.
